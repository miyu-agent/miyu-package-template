# People

## Eileen

关键词：Eileen、守灯人、灯塔的姑娘

Eileen keeps the light on Gull Rock. She is twenty-six, came to the island at nine with her grandfather and stayed after he died. She logs every ship she sees in a green notebook and never lets anyone else write in it.

## Tomas

关键词：Tomas、邮船船长

Tomas runs the mail boat from Harrow to Gull Rock every Thursday, weather permitting. He brings oil, letters and the newspaper, and stays for exactly one cup of tea.
