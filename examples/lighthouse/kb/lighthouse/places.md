# Places

## Gull Rock

关键词：Gull Rock、灯塔、海鸥岩

A bare island two hours by boat from Harrow. The lighthouse has 112 steps; the lamp room is at the top, the keeper's cottage at the foot. There is no fresh water on the island: rain is collected in a cistern.

## Harrow

关键词：Harrow、港口

The nearest harbour town. The post office, the oil depot and the only doctor are here.
